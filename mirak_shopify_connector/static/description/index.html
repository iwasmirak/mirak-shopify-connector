Simple configuration

One form for your store, with every option in plain words.

Setup in 4 steps

  1. In the Shopify Dev Dashboard, create an app with the Admin API access scopes listed in README.md, release a version and install it on your store.
  2. In Odoo, open Mirak Connector > Stores and create your store with its .myshopify.com domain, the app's Client ID and Client Secret.
  3. Click Test Connection, then choose the Shopify location and the Odoo warehouse.
  4. Choose your options and click Sync Now. Existing products are matched by SKU; the rest is created.

Requirements

  • Odoo 19, Community or Enterprise (Sales and Inventory), self-hosted or Odoo.sh.
  • A Shopify store and an app created in the Shopify Dev Dashboard.

Limits

  • Up to 3 options and 100 variants per product, as in Shopify.
  • Orders of up to 50 lines.
  • Products tracked by lot or serial number are not supported for stock.
